# Horizon Engine

A sky and landscape studio in a single HTML file. Compose physically based skies, water, grass, trees, terrain and space scenes in real time, then save them as wallpapers up to 8K or as timelapse video.

**Live:** [horizon-engine.com](https://horizon-engine.com)

## Features

- **Sky:** physically based atmosphere (Rayleigh, Mie and ozone) from noon through twilight to full night, with sun disk, lens glow, light pollution and airglow
- **Clouds:** three raymarched layers, from cumulus to cirrus, with weather-driven variation, wind, evolution, silver linings and sunset glow
- **Stars:** a deep star field with colour, size variation, diffraction spikes and star trails
- **Space:** the Milky Way with band styles and tilt, emission nebulae, a spiral galaxy, and up to two planets or moons (ringed gas giants, ice giants, rocky, ocean and lava worlds)
- **Night:** moon phases with halo and earthshine, aurora curtains and meteors
- **Land:** water with spectral waves and sun or moon glitter paths; traced grass, wheat and wildflowers; foreground trees with shadows and reflections; raymarched dunes, snow and rock; a 3D far shore of eroded hills or snowy mountains rising range behind range, a forest of individual trees, or a city with lit windows and aviation beacons; a snow-capped volcano, fog and haze
- **Motion:** timelapse of sun, moon, stars, planets and clouds, from real time up to three hours per second
- **Color:** exposure, four tone curves, white balance, split toning, vignette and film grain
- **Capture:** any aspect ratio or device size; PNG, JPEG or supersampled stills up to 8192 px; MP4 video up to 3840 px
- **Looks:** 24 built-in presets, plus looks you save yourself

## Open It

Open `index.html` in a browser with WebGL2: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari 17 or newer, on desktop or mobile. There is no build step and no server.

## Controls

- Drag to look around; scroll or pinch to zoom
- Option-drag (Alt-drag) to place the sun
- **H** hides the panel, **C** opens capture, **Space** plays the timelapse, **[** and **]** change its speed
- **⌘Z** and **⇧⌘Z** (Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z) undo and redo
